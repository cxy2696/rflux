# Code of conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant principles: be respectful,
assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and do not harass or
discriminate.

Unacceptable behaviour includes personal attacks, sustained disruption,
publishing private information without consent and any conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional research or engineering environment.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security
or maintainer contact channel.  Maintainers may remove comments, close
discussions or block contributors who violate these expectations.
